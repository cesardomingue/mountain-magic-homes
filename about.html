Our Story

A Family-Run Retreat

One mountain home, cared for personally by the family that built it — in the heart of Virginia's Blue Ridge.

Our Beginning

Built From Experience, Not Trends

Mountain Magic Homes was created from a simple belief: a true mountain retreat should feel just as exceptional as the landscape around it.

Every detail of the home was chosen intentionally to create the kind of experience we would personally want for our own family and friends.

From Brooklinen bedding and thoughtfully designed gathering spaces to the Traeger smoker and Weber grilling setup, spa amenities, oversized kitchen, mountain-view pool area, sauna, cold plunge, and expansive fire pit — the home was designed to bring people together comfortably and naturally.

Luxury here is not about being excessive or formal.

It is about comfort that feels effortless.
Spaces that feel intentional.
And creating an experience where people can slow down, reconnect, and enjoy the mountains without compromise.

Nestled in Virginia's Shenandoah Valley, Mountain Magic Homes was designed to feel elevated yet welcoming, luxurious yet relaxed, and memorable long after your stay ends.

What Guides Us

Our Principles

Three commitments we make to every guest, every stay, every season.

Designed for Gathering

Large indoor and outdoor living spaces were intentionally created to bring people together — whether around the fire pit, pool, kitchen, sauna, or mountain-view decks.

Luxury in the Details

Premium bedding, elevated furnishings, high-end grilling equipment, spa amenities, and thoughtful design choices create a stay that feels comfortable from the moment you arrive.

The Mountains Come First

Every space was designed to complement the surrounding Shenandoah Valley landscape, creating a retreat that feels connected to the views, the pace, and the atmosphere of the mountains.

The Owners

The Dominguez Family

Mountain Magic Homes is a privately owned Shenandoah Valley retreat created by the Dominguez family with one goal: build the kind of mountain getaway we always searched for, but rarely found.

What began as a vision for a better mountain retreat became a deeply personal project centered around comfort, quality, and connection.

Every improvement, furnishing, and gathering space reflects that philosophy — creating a place designed to feel warm, elevated, and unforgettable for every guest who visits.
