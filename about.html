Our Story

A Family-Run Retreat

One mountain home, cared for personally by the family that built it — in the heart of Virginia's Blue Ridge.

Our Beginning

Born of a Quiet Conviction

Mountain Magic Homes was founded on a simple idea: that a mountain retreat should feel less like a transaction and more like staying in the home of a thoughtful friend — one who knows where the light hits the kitchen at sunset and which trail is worth the walk.

We remain deliberately small — one home, one family. The property is personally cared for before every stay. Every guest reaches a real person, not a call center. Every decision, from the linens to the books on the shelf, is made with the same care we'd put into our own home.

We aren't trying to be the biggest. We're trying to be quietly, uncompromisingly, the best at this one thing.

What Guides Us

Our Principles

Three commitments we make to every guest, every stay, every season.

Considered Design

Architecture, interiors, the small ritual objects — all chosen with intention. If it doesn't earn its place, it doesn't stay.

Place Above All

The mountains are the point. The home is sited and styled to deepen, not distract from, where you actually are.

Genuine Hospitality

No scripts. No call centers. A real person who knows the property and your stay, available before, during, and after.

The Owners

Gilberto & Margret D.

Mountain Magic is owned and personally cared for by the D. family of Elkton, Virginia. They built this home with the same conviction they bring to every guest who stays in it — that a place in the mountains should feel like coming home, not checking in.
