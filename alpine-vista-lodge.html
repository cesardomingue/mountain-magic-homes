★ 4.96 (48 reviews) • Blue Ridge Mountains, Virginia • Superhost

About the Lodge

Set on twelve private acres at 2,400 feet of elevation, Alpine Vista Lodge is the kind of place that recalibrates you within an hour of arrival. Floor-to-ceiling glass walls dissolve the boundary between living room and ridgeline. A four-sided stone fireplace anchors the great room. Outside, a heated infinity-edge hot tub looks west over a hundred miles of forested peaks.

Architecture by Studio Northbound. Interiors by the late, great Wren & Field. Every linen, every cup, every chair has been chosen — and replaced, and chosen again — until it earned its place.

The Setting

Twelve private acres bordering George Washington National Forest. Three trailheads originate from the property. The nearest town — Sperryville, with its galleries and the celebrated Three Blacksmiths — is fourteen minutes by car. Dulles International is two hours; Charlottesville one.
