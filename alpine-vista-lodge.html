About the Home

A masterfully designed mountain retreat featuring panoramic vistas, a spacious kitchen, a private hot tub, a sauna, and a pool. Every detail is crafted for the discerning traveler.

The Setting

Tucked into the foothills of Stanley, Virginia — at the base of the Massanutten ridgeline and a short drive from Shenandoah National Park's Skyline Drive. The town of Luray, with its caverns and historic Main Street, is twelve minutes away. Dulles International is two hours east; Charlottesville one hour south.
