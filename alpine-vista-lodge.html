About the Lodge

Alpine Vista Lodge is built around what makes this corner of the Blue Ridge worth coming to in the first place — the view. A private pool faces the ridgeline directly, so the water and the mountains seem to meet at the horizon. The grounds are dense with mature gardens, intentionally landscaped to slow you down on the walk from the drive to the front door.

A separate guest cabin sits at the edge of the property — ideal for hosting friends, family, or a small gathering with the host. Room enough here for quiet — and room enough for company.

The Setting

Tucked into the foothills of Stanley, Virginia — at the base of the Massanutten ridgeline and a short drive from Shenandoah National Park's Skyline Drive. The town of Luray, with its caverns and historic Main Street, is twelve minutes away. Dulles International is two hours east; Charlottesville one hour south.
